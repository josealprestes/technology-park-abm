# Research plan

## Main question

Under which conditions do interactions in a technology park produce increasing returns, and when do congestion, concentration, or institutional weakness reverse those gains?

## Hypotheses

- H1: A critical mass of complementary agents increases productive partnership formation.
- H2: Diversity produces stronger innovation when sectors are technologically complementary.
- H3: Anchor firms create positive spillovers only when they actively participate in the network.
- H4: Connection infrastructure and shared facilities outperform indiscriminate entry subsidies on innovation outcomes.
- H5: Increasing returns eventually face a congestion threshold.
- H6: More distributed networks are more resilient to the departure of a central agent. Tested by E7 (distributedness axis x recovery after a central-agent departure); E6 only varied shock presence.

## Primary outcomes

- Survival and growth of startups.
- Productive partnership rate.
- Knowledge stock and diffusion.
- Innovation output.
- Investment attracted.
- Qualified employment.
- Network centralization and diversity.
- Recovery after shocks.

## Important distinction

The number of colocated organizations is not equivalent to an innovation ecosystem. The model must measure effective interaction, tie quality, compatibility, and outcomes rather than only occupancy.

## Guided investigation and hypothesis boundaries

The optional workbench makes exploratory contrasts accessible; it does not
replace the registered E1–E7 protocols. H1 receives a new size contrast;
H2's guided pair holds diversity fixed and changes complementarity; H3 varies
anchor participation. H4/H5 contrasts inspect instruments/capacity without
equal-budget or infrastructure-cost evaluation. Shock/no-shock illustrates a
perturbation, while H6 requires distributedness and recovery analysis in E7.

University links, funding, turnover and aggregate initialization are
complementary investigations, not new registered hypotheses. Each dossier
states observable behavior, counterevidence, literature application and
representation limits. Selection review: `docs/scenario-sota-review.md`;
catalog: `docs/prototypes/investigation-scenarios.json`. These UI changes do
not revise H1–H6 or promote outcomes to empirical evidence.
